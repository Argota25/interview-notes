How to get the transcript from Microsoft Teams
  1. In the meeting, click More (...), then Record and transcribe, then Start transcription.
  2. After the call, open the meeting chat or the event in your Teams calendar, then Recap, then Transcript.
  3. Click Download and choose .vtt or .docx. Drop that file here.

If Start transcription is missing, your IT admin controls that setting. Only the organizer can download the transcript; candidates who join as guests cannot.

Interview guide: Business Development Specialist

Ask the checked questions in this order for every candidate in the same opening. Start with the two warm-up questions (not scored). Ask the probes when an answer has no personal action, no result or no number. The tool checks coverage against this list after each interview. Score 2 or 4 when an answer falls between the written anchors.

Questions and anchors are designed from SHRM emotional intelligence questions, Duckworth's grit research, Dweck's growth mindset work, HEXACO Honesty-Humility, Greenlit's coachability rubric, Prospeo's mock call rubric and Bridge Group benchmarks. They are a starting point, not a validated test. Swap in Rhythm's own definitions of each value when you have them.
