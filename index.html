How to get the transcript from Microsoft Teams
  1. When the interview starts, tell the candidate the call is being transcribed for notes.
  2. In the meeting, click More (...), then Record and transcribe, then Start transcription. Teams shows everyone a notice that transcription is on.
  3. After the call, open the meeting chat or the event in your Teams calendar and go to Recap, then Transcript.
  4. Click Download and choose .vtt or .docx, then drop that file here.

If you don't see Start transcription, your IT admin controls that setting.
